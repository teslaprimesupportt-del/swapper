## Product Owner's Key Decisions (from Discovery Round 1)

1. **Name**: Working name "AI REALTIME STUDIO" — final brand TBD after architecture
2. **Experience**: Professional realtime studio — instant, cinematic, responsive, premium
3. **Providers**: No lock — research first, provider abstraction mandatory
4. **Platforms**: Desktop + Mobile from day one — not desktop-first-then-shrink
5. **Priority**: Best balance of quality + latency + reliability + cost
6. **Scale**: 10-50 concurrent MVP, architecture scales to 1000+
7. **Design**: Premium cinematic dark creative studio (not generic SaaS, not Decart clone)
8. **Mandatory features**: Video transform, face/character, appearance, clothing, background, style, VFX, reference image, voice conversion (NOT TTS), lip sync, recording, export, streaming-ready
9. **Infra**: No provider lock — research and compare before committing
10. **Timeline**: Quality over speed — incremental, no shortcuts
